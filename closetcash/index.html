closet cash

Resale profit tracker for iPhone and Android

Closet Cash is for people who sell clothes on Depop, Vinted, eBay, Poshmark, Mercari and Grailed. Log what you paid, mark pieces sold, and it works out your real profit after fees, postage and costs.

What it does

Closet and sold lists with photos · fee estimates for each app and region, including Depop boosts · thrift hauls that split the cost across pieces · monthly profit goal · levels, badges, streaks and weekly challenges · share cards for your stories · stats and CSV export.

Closet Cash Pro

Closet Cash is free to use. Closet Cash Pro adds profit by brand, style and category, boost analysis, expenses, CSV export, every card style and no watermark. It costs $2.99/month, $19.99/year (with a 1-week free trial) or $29.99 once. Prices may vary by country.

Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on iPhone, or Play Store › Payments & subscriptions on Android.
