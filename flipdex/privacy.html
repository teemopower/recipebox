Privacy Policy

Flipdex: Resale Profit Tracker for iPhone · Effective November 13, 2026

Flipdex keeps your business records yours. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't have accounts, and we don't track you.

What we collect

Nothing. Flipdex has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your inventory, sales, costs, expenses or photos.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone and, if iCloud sync is on, in your own private iCloud account so it syncs between your devices. Apple runs iCloud under its own privacy policy. We cannot access your iCloud data. You can turn sync off in Settings inside the app.

Camera and photos

Flipdex uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo of an item or check a card's centering. Photos you pick are chosen through Apple's photo picker, so Flipdex only sees the photos you select. Centering is measured on your device; photos are never uploaded to us.

Exports

CSV exports are created on your device and go only where you choose to share them.

Links to marketplaces

"Check sold prices" opens eBay, TCGplayer or Poshmark search results in your browser. Flipdex sends nothing to those sites except the search words shown. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase all data inside Flipdex to delete everything on this device and in your iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone; you can also remove Flipdex's iCloud data in the iOS Settings app under your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Purchases

Flipdex Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes payments; we receive no payment details.

Children

Flipdex does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
