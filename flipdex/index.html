Flipdex

Card Profit Tracker for iPhone

Flipdex is a bookkeeping app for trading card resellers. Log what you buy and sell, and it works out marketplace fees, shipping and your real profit and ROI.

What it does

Inventory for raw cards, graded slabs and sealed product · fee estimates for eBay, Whatnot, TCGplayer, Mercari and PayPal · lot cost splitting · grading submission tracker · expenses and mileage · profit and loss reports with CSV export · a centering checker.

Support

Questions, bugs or feature ideas: hangrygrogu@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Subscriptions

Flipdex is free for up to 25 cards. Flipdex Pro (.99/month or 9.99/year, with a free trial) unlocks unlimited inventory and the business tools. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Privacy policy

Flipdex is an independent tool and isn't affiliated with any card game, publisher, grading company or marketplace.
