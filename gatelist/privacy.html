privacy

Gatelist · effective November 18, 2026 · Adaptation Apps

Gatelist collects no personal data. Policies are generated on your device.

What it stores

Your draft (agents, sites, org name) is kept in the extension's local storage so it's there next time. It never leaves your browser.

Optional scan

If you click "scan this browser", Gatelist asks for the "management" permission and reads your installed extension list locally to suggest agent extensions. The list isn't stored or sent anywhere.

Payments

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe under their own privacy policies; we receive only whether Pro is unlocked.
