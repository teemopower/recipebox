Cliffwright

Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026. Short version: your writing stays yours, and we don't collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Cliffwright has no account, no analytics, no ads and no servers of ours. We never see your seasons, scripts or notes.

Where your writing is stored

On Android, everything is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. If you make a backup file, it goes where you choose to save it.

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, your writing is saved on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account so it appears on your other Apple devices. Apple stores that data; we can't read it.

Writing suggestions

On supported devices, suggestions for hooks, cliffhangers and next episodes are generated by a model that runs on the device itself (Gemini Nano on Android, Apple's on-device model on Apple devices). Your text is not sent to us or to anyone else to make those suggestions.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We receive no payment details. The app only checks whether your purchase is active.

Exports and sharing

PDFs and Fountain files are created on your device. They only go where you send them using your device's share sheet.

Children

Cliffwright isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a season or episode in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on the device. On Apple devices, you can also remove the app's iCloud data in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage.
