Cliffwright

Write the episode they can't skip.

Cliffwright is a phone-first writing room for vertical micro-dramas: 60 to 90 second episodes, dozens per season, every one ending on a cliff.

What it does

Season board. Every episode is a phone-shaped card, with the paywall episode marked, so you see the whole season's rhythm at a glance.
Hook, twist, cliff. Plan the three beats of each episode before you write a line.
Runtime meter. Screen time is estimated as you type, so each episode lands between 45 and 95 seconds.
Beat checks. Does the hook land in three seconds? Does it end on an open question, not a fade-out?
Storylines. Track every thread across the season and get a nudge when one goes quiet.
Pitch-ready exports. Screenplay PDFs, Fountain files and a pitch pack with your logline, cast and episode grid.
