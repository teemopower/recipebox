privacy

Field Mask · effective November 18, 2026 · Adaptation Apps

Field Mask collects no personal data. It runs entirely in your browser.

What it reads

To decide what to blur, Field Mask looks at form fields and (if you turn on page-text scanning) visible text on pages you open. This happens locally. Nothing from the page is stored or sent anywhere.

What it stores

Your settings and the elements you picked to hide are saved with your browser's extension storage, which your browser may sync to your own account.

Payments

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. If you buy it, they handle your email and payment details under their own privacy policies; we receive only whether Pro is unlocked.
