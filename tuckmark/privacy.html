Tuckmark

Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026 (phone apps added)

Tuckmark is a browser extension and an iPhone and Android app for keeping private links in an encrypted vault. It is built so that we never receive your links, titles, notes, tags, passphrases or browsing activity.

What stays on your device

  • Your links, titles, notes and tags are encrypted with your passphrase (Argon2id key derivation, AES-256-GCM) and stored only in your browser's extension storage on this device.
  • Your settings (theme, auto-lock time, how links open, the No trace in history and screen-lock options), a count and the times of wrong passphrase attempts used for the break-in alert, and your licence key if you buy Pro.
  • Backups you export are encrypted files saved wherever you choose. We never see them.

Tuckmark has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no servers of its own. It does not sync, and nothing is uploaded.

The one network request

If you buy Tuckmark Pro and enter a licence key, Tuckmark asks your permission to contact our payment provider, Polar (polar.sh), and then sends only the licence key, our public organization ID and a label naming your browser (for example "Tuckmark · Edge") to activate, check or remove the key. It sends nothing about your vault. Purchases happen on Polar's checkout page, and Polar handles your payment and email under its own privacy policy. We receive the email address and order details Polar shares with sellers, only to provide the licence and support.

The decoy list is filled from site lists built into the extension. It does not download anything.

Permissions and why

  • Storage and unlimited storage: to keep the encrypted vault and settings on your device.
  • Active tab: to read the address and title of the page you're on, only when you open Tuckmark or press the save shortcut, so you can add it.
  • Idle: to lock Tuckmark when your screen locks or the computer goes idle.
  • History (optional, asked only if you turn on No trace in history): to remove the addresses you open from Tuckmark from your browser history. Tuckmark doesn't read or collect your history.
  • api.polar.sh (optional, asked only when you enter a licence key): to check the licence key as described above.

The iPhone and Android apps

  • Storage: the same encrypted vault format, kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is excluded from iCloud and Google backups. The iPhone share sheet reads it from a container only Tuckmark can use.
  • Link previews (on by default, can be turned off): when you save a link, your phone visits that page once to read its title and preview picture. The picture is shrunk and stored inside your encrypted list. The site sees a normal visit from your phone; we see nothing.
  • Private browser: pages you open inside Tuckmark use a separate browser session whose cookies, cache and history are thrown away when Tuckmark locks. Sites you visit still see the visit, and your network or employer may too. When you type words that aren't a web address into its address bar, they are sent as a search to DuckDuckGo (DuckDuckGo's privacy policy), the same as searching there yourself.
  • Daily browsing count: the free version counts how many private browsing sessions you start each day (a date and a number, stored only on your phone) to apply the free limit. It records nothing about the pages you visit.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint (optional): if you turn it on, the passphrase for the list you are in is stored in your phone's secure keychain behind your biometrics. Your face or fingerprint data never reaches Tuckmark.
  • Motion (optional): used only on your phone to lock Tuckmark when you flip it face-down or shake it. Nothing is recorded.
  • Purchases: Tuckmark Pro on phones is bought through the App Store or Google Play. They handle payment and tell the app only whether you own Pro.

Selling or sharing data

We don't sell, rent or share your data, because we don't have it. We don't use it for advertising, credit decisions or anything unrelated to Tuckmark.

Deleting your data

Use Erase everything on this device in Tuckmark's settings, or remove the extension; your browser deletes its storage. To remove licence activations, use Deactivate on this browser in Settings or contact us.

Children

Tuckmark is not directed at children under 13.
