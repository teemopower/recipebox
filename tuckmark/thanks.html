Thanks for getting Tuckmark Pro

  1. Check your email for your licence key (it starts with TUCK).
  2. Open Tuckmark, unlock it, and go to Settings, Tuckmark Pro.
  3. Paste the key and choose Activate. Allow the one-time permission to check it with Polar.

The key works on up to 3 browsers. Can't find it? Email hangrygrogu@gmail.com with your order email.
