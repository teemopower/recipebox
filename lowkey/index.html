faq

Does it really run on my computer?

Yes. lowkey uses the AI built into Microsoft Edge (Phi-4-mini) and Google Chrome (Gemini Nano). The first time, your browser downloads the model once in the background.

What if my computer can't run it?

Some older or smaller computers can't. Pro includes cloud boosts that work anywhere. Those are processed and sent right back, never stored or used for training.

Is it allowed at work?

Check your company's rules, but on-device AI means your text doesn't go to an outside AI service, which is usually the main concern. The Teams plan adds a privacy report you can hand to IT.
