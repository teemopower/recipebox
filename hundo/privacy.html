Privacy Policy

Hundo: Game 100% Tracker for Android · Effective November 13, 2026

Hundo keeps your checklists and progress on your phone. There are no accounts and we don't run servers that store your data.

Your checklists

Everything you check off, pin, note or add is stored in the app on your device and included in your device's normal backups. Deleting the app deletes it. We never see it.

Checklist updates

To get new checklist items, the app downloads list files from our website (teemopower.github.io, hosted by GitHub). Like any website, GitHub may log basic request details such as your IP address. No personal data is sent with these requests.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about ad interactions, and may use cookies or similar technology. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Hundo Pro removes ads; Pro users don't load the ads SDK.

Purchases

Hundo Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we receive no payment details.

Children

Hundo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Unofficial app

Hundo is an unofficial, fan-made tracker. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any game publisher. Game names are trademarks of their respective owners.
