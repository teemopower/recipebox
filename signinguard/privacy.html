Sign-in Guard

Privacy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Adaptation Apps

Sign-in Guard collects no personal data. It runs entirely in your browser and has no server.

What it reads

When your browser opens a Google or Microsoft sign-in address, Sign-in Guard reads the permission list in that address (for example “read your email”) and the site you’ll return to, so it can explain them. On other pages it looks for a fake sign-in window drawn inside the page. This happens locally. It never reads or stores your password, and nothing from any page is sent anywhere.

What it stores

A short list of your last 30 checks (time, Google or Microsoft, the return site and the risk level) is kept in your browser’s local extension storage so you can see it in the popup. Your settings and any sites you marked as fine are saved in your browser’s extension storage, which your browser may sync to your own account. Uninstalling removes all of it.

Sharing

None. No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
