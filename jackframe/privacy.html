Jackframe privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Adaptation Apps

Short version: Jackframe doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

Your stars, best scores, daily results and streak, hint count and settings (sound, vibration, theme) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Android may include them in your own device backup if you have backup turned on.

Purchases

The optional Night Pass is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app whether you own the pass. We never see your payment details. Google's handling of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

When you tap "share result", the app opens Android's share menu with a short text (your score and an emoji grid). Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself.

Permissions

Jackframe uses vibration for game feedback and network access only so Google Play billing can work. It does not use location, contacts, the camera, the microphone or advertising IDs.

Children

Jackframe doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: hangrygrogu@gmail.com.
