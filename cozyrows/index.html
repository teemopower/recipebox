cozy rows

Crochet and knitting tracker for iPhone and Android

Cozy Rows keeps count for you. Tap to count rows, time your sessions, keep your pattern open with a row highlighter, and see your yarn stash, streaks and finished makes in one place.

What it does

Big tap-anywhere row counter · extra counters with auto-repeat · session timer · PDF patterns with a draggable highlighter · yarn stash · pricing for makes you sell (materials plus your time) · streaks, levels, badges and weekly challenges · share cards for your stories · stats and backups.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: hangrygrogu@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Cozy Rows Pro

Cozy Rows is free to use with up to 3 projects in progress and 5 yarns. Cozy Rows Pro adds unlimited projects and stash, pattern PDFs on every project, pricing and sales tracking, full stats history, every card style and no watermark. It costs $2.99/month, $14.99/year (with a 1-week free trial) or $24.99 once. Prices may vary by country.
