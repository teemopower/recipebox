Privacy Policy

Cozy Rows for iPhone and Android · Effective November 12, 2026

Cozy Rows keeps your projects, counts and stash on your phone. We don't run servers that store your data, there are no accounts, and we don't track you.

What we collect

Nothing. Cozy Rows has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your projects, counts, yarn, patterns or photos.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored in the app on your device and is included in your device's normal backups. Deleting the app deletes the data. You can also erase it from Settings inside the app.

Pattern files

PDF patterns you import are copied into the app on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Camera and photos

Cozy Rows uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo of a project or yarn. Photos stay on your device. If you save a share card, the app asks to add it to your photos.

Sharing and exports

Share cards and backup files are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them.

Purchases

Cozy Rows Pro is sold through the App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google process payments; we receive no payment details.

Children

Cozy Rows does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update this page and the date above.
