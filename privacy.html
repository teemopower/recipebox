Privacy Policy

Recipe Box for iPhone · Effective November 10, 2026

Recipe Box is made to keep your recipes yours. We don't run servers that store your recipes, we don't have accounts, and we don't track you.

What we collect

Nothing. Recipe Box has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your recipes, photos, grocery lists or meal plans.

Where your recipes live

Your recipes are stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account, which syncs them between your devices. Apple runs iCloud under its own privacy policy. We cannot access your iCloud data.

Family cookbooks

When you share a cookbook, Apple's iCloud sharing makes it available to the people you invite. They can see the cookbook's recipes and photos, and the names that Apple shows for participants. You can stop sharing or remove people at any time.

Recipe links

When you share a recipe as a link, the recipe's text is packed into the link itself. Opening the link shows the recipe on this website without sending the recipe to any server; the website has no analytics or cookies. Anyone who has the link can read the recipe, so share it only with people you want to see it.

Importing and scanning

When you save a recipe from a web page, your iPhone downloads that page directly from its website. Scanned pages are read on your device and are not uploaded. Smart import uses Apple Intelligence on your device where available.

Purchases

Recipe Box Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple processes payments; we receive no payment details.

Children

Recipe Box does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
